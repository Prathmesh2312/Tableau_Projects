# Sales & Customer Dashboard - Tableau Project

## 📊 Project Overview

This Tableau project consists of two interactive dashboards — **Sales Dashboard** and **Customer Dashboard** — built to support decision-making for sales managers, executives, and marketing teams. The dashboards provide a high-level overview as well as detailed insights into sales performance, product trends, customer behavior, and engagement metrics.

---

## 👤 User Story

> As a stakeholder, I want interactive dashboards that help analyze sales performance and customer trends, so I can make data-driven decisions and improve business outcomes.

---

## 🚀 Dashboards & Features

### 📈 Sales Dashboard

#### 🔹 Purpose
To visualize sales metrics and trends for analyzing year-over-year performance and identifying key patterns in sales data.

#### 🔸 Key Features

- **KPI Overview**  
  - Total Sales, Profit, and Quantity for the current and previous year.

- **Sales Trends (Monthly)**  
  - Monthly breakdown of KPIs for both years.
  - Clear visual indication of months with **highest** and **lowest** sales.

- **Product Subcategory Comparison**  
  - Compare subcategory sales performance year-over-year.
  - Include **sales vs. profit** comparison to assess margins.

- **Weekly Sales & Profit Trends**  
  - Weekly data visualization for current year.
  - Highlight weeks **above/below** average performance.

---

### 👥 Customer Dashboard

#### 🔹 Purpose
To provide insights into customer metrics and behaviors to support marketing strategies and customer retention initiatives.

#### 🔸 Key Features

- **KPI Overview**  
  - Total number of customers, average sales per customer, and total orders (YTD vs previous year).

- **Customer Trends (Monthly)**  
  - Monthly KPIs for both years.
  - Highlight best and worst months in terms of sales.

- **Customer Order Distribution**  
  - Visual distribution of customers based on number of orders to analyze loyalty and engagement.

- **Top 10 Customers by Profit**  
  - List of top 10 customers by profit.
  - Show rank, order count, sales, profit, and last order date.

---

## 🎨 Design & Interactivity

- **Dynamic Year Selection**  
  - Users can select and compare data for different years.

- **Seamless Navigation**  
  - Switch easily between Sales and Customer dashboards.

- **Interactive Visuals**  
  - Clickable charts and graphs for filtering and deeper exploration.

- **Data Filters**  
  - Filter data by:
    - **Product Info**: Category, Subcategory  
    - **Location Info**: Region, State, City

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Tableau** for data visualization
- **Sales and Customer Data** (synthetic or anonymized for privacy)

## 📁 How to View

To explore the dashboards:

1. Download the `.twbx` file from this repository.
2. Open with [Tableau Desktop](https://www.tableau.com/products/desktop) or Tableau Public.
3. Interact with filters, KPIs, and visualizations for full insight.
